    Model & Methodology

    How Vitalis turns patient vitals into a risk profile.

    01 — Data

    Trained on 5,000 synthetic patient records with realistic correlations between age, BMI, blood pressure, glucose, cholesterol, and lifestyle factors. Generated for demonstration — not real patient data.

    02 — Risk level classifier

    A Random Forest classifier (300 trees) predicts Low / Medium / High overall risk from 14 engineered features, reaching 85% held-out accuracy.

    03 — Condition-specific models

    Two Gradient Boosting regressors estimate diabetes risk and cardiovascular risk independently, each scored 0–100%.

    04 — Recommendations engine

    A clinical rules layer converts out-of-range vitals into plain-language, prioritized next steps alongside the model's output.
